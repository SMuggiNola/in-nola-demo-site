About IN-NOLA

About us →

Irish Network New Orleans is part of Irish Network USA, run by volunteers who wanted the Irish side of this city to have somewhere to gather. We put on events, keep the history, and make room for anybody curious about it.

Who we areWhat the network does and how it started. The boardThe volunteers running it, and how to reach them. Help improve this siteTell us what's clumsy or broken. We fix it together.
