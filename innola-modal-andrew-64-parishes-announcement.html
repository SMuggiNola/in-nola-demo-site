An Irish Republican in Louisiana

by IN-NOLA Board Member Andrew R. Jones

IN-NOLA is proud to share a new feature article by our own Andrew R. Jones, telling the remarkable story of J.F.X. O'Brien — Irish nationalist, Civil War surgeon, political prisoner, and resident of New Orleans and Baton Rouge from 1857 to 1862. His story is a vivid chapter in the deep, enduring connection between Ireland and Louisiana.
